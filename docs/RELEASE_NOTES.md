## Download Picture Book Writer

**Windows:** download **`Picture-Book-Writer-Setup-…exe`** below, then double-click it. It installs by itself, puts a **Picture Book Writer** icon on your Desktop, and opens the app.

> If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. (The app isn't signed with a paid Microsoft certificate, so Windows shows this for new apps.)

**Mac:** download **`Picture-Book-Writer-…-Mac.dmg`** below and double-click it. Drag **Picture Book Writer** into the **Applications** folder. The first time you open it, **right-click** (or Control-click) the app and choose **Open**, then click **Open** again.

> On newer Macs you may need to go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. (The app isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate.)

Your books are saved on your **Desktop** as `.pbook` files. Double-click one to open it, or open Picture Book Writer and pick it from the list.
